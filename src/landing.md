## Resources
#### [DIM](https://destinyitemmanager.com/)
Destiny Item Manager. Useful tool for creating builds and managing items in your vault. Most builds online will come with DIM links that you can use to apply them to your character with the gear you have. 
#### [Light.gg](https://www.light.gg/)
Light.gg is a destiny database. I use it to find what the most popular perk combinations on weapons. I also use it to find what weapons are popular and where to get them from. Popular weapons are typically good weapons.

#### [Destiny.report](https://destiny.report/)
Destiny.report is another destiny database, but more focused on weapons. I use it to find weapons that synergize with my builds or for niche situations.

For example, I typically run a solar hunter build for the caretaker encounter in Vow of the Disciple, and I am usually on stun duty. I wanted a gun that has incandescent to synergize with my build, has good range to help clear ads between stuns, deals anti-barrier damage for the vandals, and with heal clip for better survivability. Very niche, but searching [`perk1:healclip perk2:incandescent breaker:barrier ammo:primary`](https://destiny.report/?q=breaker%3Abarrier+perk1%3A%22heal+clip%22+perk2%3Aincandescent+is%3Aprimary) led me to the pulse rifle "adhortative" or the handcannon "epochal integration".

#### [Destiny Power Bars](https://destiny-power-bars.corke.dev/)
Power bars is a tool to track your power level, and identify which pieces of gear to focus to level up faster.

#### [Stacking Buffs/Debuffs Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i1KUwgVkd8qhwYj481gkV9sZNJQCE-C3Q-dpQutPCi4/edit?gid=2073397587#gid=2073397587)
This google sheet explains the various different types of buffs and debuffs that you can stack to maximize your damage.
